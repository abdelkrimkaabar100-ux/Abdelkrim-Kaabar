## 🧠 Who I Am

I am a **psychologist and AI researcher** at [Ibn Tofail University](https://www.uit.ac.ma), Kénitra, Morocco.  
My work sits at the intersection of **psychology, philosophy of mind, and artificial intelligence** — exploring how deep psychological understanding can shape safer, more human-aligned AI systems.

I am also the **founder of [Soon](https://www.soonpsy.com)** — a psycho-digital application dedicated to making psychological support accessible to both **humans and animals**, built on principles of privacy, self-direction, and zero-cost access.

## 💡 Philosophy

> *"The goal is not to fix the mind — but to understand it, deeply and without judgment."*

I believe that:
- **Psychology** is the missing layer in AGI alignment research
- **The human psyche (النفس)** is a more precise descriptor of humanity than "intelligence"
- **Democratic, zero-cost access** to psychological support is a right, not a privilege
- **Animals** have a psychological life that deserves recognition and research
